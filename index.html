ACCOUNT SECURITY

Password Hygiene

Good password habits are your first line of digital defence.

🔑

Use Long Passwords

Choose long, hard-to-guess passwords or passphrases. Avoid names, birthdays, and predictable patterns.

🔄

Never Reuse Passwords

Use a different password for every important account. Reusing passwords can put multiple accounts at risk.

🔐

Enable MFA

Multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection beyond your password.

🗝️

Use a Password Manager

A reputable password manager can create and store unique passwords securely.

🚫

Keep Passwords Secret

Never share your password or OTP with callers, strangers, or people claiming to be support staff.

⚠️

Act on Suspicious Activity

If an account may be compromised, change its password using the official website and review account sessions.

PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION

Your Data. Your Responsibility.

Small privacy habits can make a big difference.

📱

Review App Permissions

Allow camera, microphone, location, and contacts access only when needed.

👤

Limit Personal Sharing

Avoid publicly posting your address, identity documents, travel plans, or sensitive personal details.

🌐

Browse Safely

Use trusted websites, check the domain, and avoid entering sensitive information through suspicious links.

📶

Secure Your Devices

Keep software updated, lock your devices, and use trusted networks for sensitive activities.

🗑️

Delete Responsibly

Remove unnecessary personal information and securely dispose of old documents and storage devices.

💾

Back Up Important Data

Keep suitable backups of important files and protect access to those backups.

THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

Recognise Online Scams

Pause, verify, and report suspicious activity.

📧

Phishing Messages

Fake emails or messages may imitate banks, delivery services, or trusted organisations to steal information.

VERIFY LINKS
📲

OTP and UPI Fraud

Never disclose an OTP, PIN, or password. Verify payment requests and remember that receiving money via UPI does not require entering your UPI PIN.

NEVER SHARE OTPs
🎭

Impersonation Scams

Scammers may pretend to be customer care agents, officials, friends, or family members. Verify independently.

VERIFY IDENTITY
📥

Suspicious Downloads

Avoid unknown attachments, unofficial apps, and unexpected files that request unnecessary permissions.
